Privacy Policy for Bike Rush

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bike Rush ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you play Bike Rush (the "Game"), available on Google Play under the app ID com.ravikarnangame.bikerush.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or receive any personal information from the Game. The Game has no accounts and no registration, and we do not run any server that receives data from it.

  • Personal data: We do not ask for your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • Location data: We do not track, collect, or store your location.
  • Camera, microphone, photos, and contacts: The Game does not request or access them.
  • Advertising and analytics: The Game shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

3. Local Data Storage

To make the Game work, it saves small amounts of data on your device: your best score, your coin balance, the bikes you own and the one you have selected, whether you have the Double Coins upgrade, and the purchase IDs needed to avoid crediting a coin pack twice.

This data is not sent to us. If you have turned on your device's backup feature, the operating system may include it in the backup stored in your own Google account. We have no access to that backup.

Clearing the Game's data or uninstalling the Game can permanently delete your best score, your coins, and bikes unlocked with coins. Bikes bought with real money and the Double Coins upgrade can be recovered at any time with "Restore purchases" in the Shop.

4. In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional purchases: coin packs, the Double Coins upgrade, and premium bikes. All purchases are processed by Google Play's billing system.

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google. We never see or store your payment card details or billing address.
  • To deliver what you bought, the Game receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play (the item and a purchase ID). It is stored only on your device.
  • As the seller, we can see order records in the Google Play Console, such as the order number, item, price, and date. We use them only for refunds and support.

Google's handling of your purchase and payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Internet Connection

The Game can be played without an internet connection. It uses the internet only for the Shop: loading item prices, and making or restoring purchases through Google Play. As part of this, the Google Play Billing software may send technical and diagnostic information to Google.

The Game does not send any data to servers of our own.

6. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service in the Game is Google Play Billing, used for in-app purchases as described above. The Game does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics tools, or frameworks that track you across other apps.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not collect personal information from any user, including children.

In-app purchases cost real money and are charged to the Google Play account on the device. Parents and guardians can require a password or approval for purchases in the Google Play settings.

If a parent or legal guardian believes their child has somehow provided us with information, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this page periodically.
