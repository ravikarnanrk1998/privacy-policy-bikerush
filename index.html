Privacy Policy for Bike Rush: Offline Moto Racing

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bike Rush: Offline Moto Racing ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game (the "Game") collects, how it is used and stored, and how you can delete it. The Game is available on Google Play under the app ID com.ravikarnangame.bikerush.

2. Information We Collect

To play, you sign in with your Google account or with an email address and password. This lets us save your progress to your account, so it is not lost if you reinstall the Game or change phones. We collect:

  • Account information: your email address and a user ID created for your account. If you sign in with an email address and password, your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it. If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication also receives your Google account's name and profile picture link. The Game does not use or show them.
  • Game progress: your coins and diamonds, best score, the bikes, cars and skins you own and the ones you use, the stages you have unlocked, the Double Coins upgrade, the cartoon avatar you pick, and the day you last claimed your free daily diamond.
  • Purchase records: the purchase IDs of items you buy, so that each purchase is delivered exactly once.
  • 1 VS 1 matches: when you play a 1 VS 1 match, your player name (the part of your email address before the "@"), your user ID and your score are shared with the other player in that match.

We do not collect:

  • Location data: we do not track, collect, or store your location.
  • Camera, microphone, photos, and contacts: the Game does not request or access them.
  • Advertising and analytics: the Game shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics tools.

3. How We Use Information

We use this information only to run the Game: to sign you in, to save your progress and bring it back on any device where you sign in, to run 1 VS 1 matches, and to deliver what you buy. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Where Your Information Is Stored

  • In your account: your account and game progress are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Other players cannot see or change your saved progress.
  • On your device: the Game also keeps a copy of your progress on your phone, so you can play without an internet connection. If your device's backup feature is on, the operating system may include it in the backup stored in your own Google account.
  • 1 VS 1 matches: a match is stored while it is played and deleted when it is closed. It holds only the two players' names, user IDs and scores.

5. In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional purchases: coin packs, diamond packs, the Double Coins upgrade, and premium bikes, cars and skins. All purchases are processed by Google Play's billing system.

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google. We never see or store your payment card details or billing address.
  • To deliver what you bought, the Game receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play (the item and a purchase ID). It is saved on your device and with your game progress in your account.
  • As the seller, we can see order records in the Google Play Console, such as the order number, item, price, and date. We use them only for refunds and support.

Google's handling of your purchase and payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Internet Connection

You need an internet connection to sign in the first time. After that, you can play races offline, and your progress is saved to your account the next time you are online. The Game also uses the internet for 1 VS 1 matches and for the Shop (loading prices, and making or restoring purchases through Google Play).

7. Third-Party Services

The Game uses these services, all provided by Google:

  • Firebase Authentication, to sign you in.
  • Cloud Firestore, to store your saved progress and 1 VS 1 matches.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Google Play Billing, for in-app purchases.

To provide these services and protect them from abuse, Google may process technical information such as your IP address and device and app details. See the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy. The Game does not include advertising SDKs, analytics tools, or anything that tracks you across other apps.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. It is shared only:

  • with the other player in a 1 VS 1 match, who sees your player name and score;
  • with Google, which stores and processes it for us as described above; and
  • if the law requires us to.

9. Deleting Your Account and Data

You can delete your account and all its saved data at any time, directly in the Game:

  1. Open the Game. On the menu, tap your email address at the top left.
  2. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  3. Confirm by entering your password, or by picking your Google account.

This permanently deletes your account and your saved progress: coins, diamonds, best score, owned bikes, cars and skins, unlocked stages and purchase records.

If you can no longer open the Game, email us at ravikarnanrk1998@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Bike Rush account". We will delete your account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Order records of past purchases are kept by Google Play under its own policies. Premium items bought with real money (bikes, cars, skins and the Double Coins upgrade) stay linked to your Google Play account and can be restored with "Restore purchases" in the Garage. Coins and diamonds cannot be restored once your account is deleted.

If you do not delete your account, your saved progress is kept so it can be restored whenever you sign in.

10. Security

Your information is sent over encrypted connections. Your saved progress is protected so that other players cannot read or change it.

11. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

In-app purchases cost real money and are charged to the Google Play account on the device. Parents and guardians can require a password or approval for purchases in the Google Play settings.

If a parent or legal guardian believes their child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this page periodically.
